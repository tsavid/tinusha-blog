## Pillar 1: Jurisdictional Scope & Applicability

| Section                         | Direct Quote                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Application to SMBs                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Section 1(1)**                | "This Act may be cited as the Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022." (p. 1, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | When referencing the law in contracts, privacy policies, or legal opinions, always cite the full statutory name.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Section 1(3)** (Amended 2025) | "All other provisions of this Act except this section, shall come into operation on such date or dates as the Minister may appoint, by Order published in the Gazette." (p. 1, Act No. 22 of 2025)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | The 2025 amendment removed the original fixed timelines (1836 months) and gives the Minister flexible power to bring provisions into operation on any date. Monitor gazette notifications to know exactly when each Part becomes enforceable against your SME.                                                                                                          |
| **Section 2(1)**                | "This Act shall apply to the processing of personal data— (a) where the processing of personal data takes place wholly or partly within Sri Lanka; or (b) where the processing of personal data is carried out by a controller or processor who (i) is domiciled or ordinarily resident in Sri Lanka; (ii) is incorporated or established under any written law of Sri Lanka; (iii) offers goods or services to data subjects in Sri Lanka including the offering of goods or services with specific targeting of data subjects in Sri Lanka; or (iv) specifically monitors the behaviour of data subjects in Sri Lanka including profiling with the intention of making decisions in relation to the behavior of such data subjects in so far as such behaviour takes place in Sri Lanka." (pp. 23, Act No. 9 of 2022) | Any local SMB incorporated in Sri Lanka or targeting Sri Lankan consumers is fully bound by this law. If you operate an e-commerce platform, local retail store, or digital service targeting local residents, you must maintain a compliance program. Even businesses incorporated abroad but offering goods/services to or monitoring Sri Lankan residents are caught. |
| **Section 2(2)**                | "For the purposes of paragraphs (iii) and (iv) of subsection (1) respectively, the Authority may, determine by way of rules made under this Act— (a) the circumstances in which the specific targeting of the data subjects may occur; or (b) the circumstances in which the specific monitoring of the data subjects may occur." (p. 3, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | The Data Protection Authority has power to define what constitutes "targeting" and "monitoring." Watch for published rules that may expand the scope of who is caught under the Act.                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Section 2(3)**                | "This Act shall not apply to (a) any personal data processed purely for personal, domestic or household purposes by an individual; and (b) any data other than personal data." (p. 3, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Business communications, B2B data that does not identify individuals, and purely personal/domestic contacts are excluded. However, all employee records, B2C customer lists, and vendor contact info fall under the law.                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Section 3(1)**                | "The provisions of this Act shall have effect notwithstanding anything to the contrary in any other written law, relating to the protection of personal data of data subjects:" (p. 3, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | The PDPA overrides other conflicting privacy terms or local industry standards. Your legal agreements, vendor contracts, and internal guidelines must be updated to align with the PDPA.                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Section 3(2)**                | "In the event of any inconsistency between the provisions of this Act and the provisions of such written law, the provisions of this Act shall prevail." (p. 3, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | If any existing law conflicts with the PDPA on personal data protection matters, the PDPA wins. Ensure your legal counsel reviews all legacy compliance frameworks against this Act.                                                                                                                                                                                     |

## Pillar 2: Definitions & Interpretive Foundations

| Section                        | Direct Quote                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Application to SMBs                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Section 56**                 | ""personal data" means any information that can identify a natural person, either directly or indirectly, by reference to an identifier (such as a name, ID number, or location data) or to one or more factors specific to the physical, physiological, genetic, psychological, economic, cultural or social identity of that individual or natural person." (p. 49, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                        | This determines the scope of your compliance. Any customer records, employee profiles, email lists, phone numbers, IP addresses, or website tracking cookies are legally personal data.                                                                 |
| **Section 56 **                | ""controller" means, any natural or legal person... which alone or jointly with others determines the purposes and means of the processing..." (p. 49, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | If your business decides why and how personal data is collected and used, you are the controller. The controller bears the heaviest compliance burden.                                                                                                  |
| **Section 56 **                | ""processor" means, a natural or legal person... which processes personal data on behalf of the controller;" (p. 49, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | If you process data on behalf of another company, you are a processor. Processors have distinct obligations under Section 22.                                                                                                                           |
| **Section 56 **                | ""processing" means, any operation or set of operations which is performed on personal data or on sets of personal data, whether or not by automated means, such as collection, recording, organisation, structuring, storage, adaptation or alteration, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure by transmission, dissemination or otherwise making available, alignment or combination, restriction, erasure or destruction;" (p. 50, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                      | Virtually everything you do with personal data from collecting it on a sign-up form to storing it in a spreadsheet, emailing it to a colleague, or deleting it constitutes "processing" and triggers the Act's obligations.                             |
| **Section 56 **                | ""special categories of personal data" means, the personal data revealing racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious or philosophical beliefs, the processing of genetic data, biometric data for the purpose of uniquely identifying a natural person, data concerning health or data concerning a natural person's sex life or sexual orientation, personal data relating to offences, criminal proceedings and convictions, or personal data relating to a child;" (pp. 5253, Act No. 9 of 2022) | Handling employee sick leave records, biometric fingerprint attendance, background checks, or children's details is classified as processing "special categories" and requires complying with Schedule II, not just Schedule I.                         |
| **Section 56 **                | ""consent" of the data subject means any freely given, specific, informed and unambiguous indication of the data subject's wishes by way of a written declaration or an affirmative action signifying a data subject's agreement to the processing of personal data relating to him or her;" (p. 49, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                         | Consent must be a positive opt-in. Silence, inactivity, pre-ticked checkboxes, or general terms-of-service bundle updates do not constitute consent. Forms must require an active user action.                                                          |
| **Section 56 **                | ""personal data breach" means, any act or omission that results in accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, personal data transmitted, stored or otherwise processed;" (p. 51, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Breaches are not limited to external hacks. Accidentally sending customer details to the wrong client, losing an unencrypted laptop, or printing out employee salaries and leaving them visible are breaches.                                           |
| **Section 56 **                | ""profiling" means, processing of personal data to evaluate, analyse or predict aspects concerning that data subject's performance at work, economic situation, health, personal preferences, interests, credibility, behavior, habits, location or movements;" (p. 51, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | If your SME uses analytics tools, credit scoring, employee performance tracking, or customer segmentation based on behavior data, you are legally engaged in "profiling" and additional obligations (DPIAs, right to review automated decisions) apply. |
| **Section 56 **                | ""pseudonymisation" means, the processing of personal data in such a manner that the personal data cannot be used to identify a data subject without the use of additional information and such additional information is kept separately and is subject to technical and organizational measures to ensure that the personal data is not attributed to a data subject;" (p. 51, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                             | Pseudonymisation is explicitly recognized as a security measure under the Act. SMEs can reduce risk by replacing customer identifiers with codes/tokens while storing the key mapping separately with restricted access.                                |
| **Section 56 **                | ""recipient" means, a natural or legal person to whom the personal data is disclosed, or a public Authority or any incorporated or unincorporated body to which the personal data is disclosed;" (p. 52, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Every person or entity you share personal data with is a "recipient." You must disclose all recipients in your privacy policy (per Schedule V) and track all disclosures in your Record of Processing Activities.                                       |
| **Section 56 **                | ""third party" means, a natural or legal person, public authority, agency or body other than the data subject, controller, processor and persons who are under the direct authority of the controller or processor, are authorized to process personal data;" (p. 53, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | If you share data with any entity that is not your employee or processor, they are "third parties" and separate legal bases and disclosures are required.                                                                                               |
| **Section 56 ** (Amended 2025) | ""Data Protection Officer" means, the person designated or appointed under section 20 and includes a third party who is not directly employed by a controller or processor, but fulfils the responsibilities under section 20(5);" (pp. 1011, Act No. 22 of 2025)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | The 2025 amendment explicitly allows SMEs to hire an external consultant or fractional DPO rather than employing a full-time in-house officer. This significantly reduces payroll overhead for smaller businesses.                                      |
| **Section 56 ** (Amended 2025) | ""third country" means, any other territory or country other than Sri Lanka;" (p. 11, Act No. 22 of 2025)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | The 2025 amendment broadened this from "a country prescribed under section 26" to any territory outside Sri Lanka. Any transfer of data to any foreign country now falls under cross-border rules.                                                      |
| **Section 56 ** (Amended 2025) | ""public authority" means, a Ministry, any Department, Provincial Council, local authority, or a Ministry or Department of a Provincial Council but does not include— (a) a public corporation; or (b) a company incorporated under the Companies Act, No. 07 of 2007;" (p. 11, Act No. 22 of 2025)                                                                                                                                                                                                             | The 2025 amendment excludes public corporations and Companies Act entities from the "public authority" definition. This means state-owned enterprises registered as companies are now treated like private sector controllers for compliance purposes.  |
| **Section 22(5) **             | "For the purposes of this section "personnel" means any employee, consultant, agent, affiliate or any person who is contracted by the processor to process personal data." (p. 17, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Confidentiality obligations extend beyond employees to consultants, agents, affiliates, and any contracted individuals. Ensure all of these sign confidentiality agreements.                                                                            |

## Pillar 3: Core Data Processing Principles (Data Governance)

| Section          | Direct Quote                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Application to SMBs                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Section 4**    | "Every controller shall process personal data in compliance with the obligations specified under this Act." (p. 3, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Even if you outsource your IT, payroll, or cloud hosting to third parties, your SMB remains the "Controller" legally responsible for ensuring the entire processing lifecycle is compliant.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Section 5**    | "The processing of personal data shall be lawful if a controller is in compliance with— (a) any condition specified in Schedule I hereto; (b) any condition specified in Schedule II hereto in the case of processing special categories of personal data; (c) all the conditions specified in Schedule III hereto in the case of processing personal data based on the consent of the data subject under item (a) of Schedule I or under item (a) of Schedule II hereto; or (d) all the conditions specified in Schedule IV hereto in the case of processing personal data in respect of criminal investigations." (pp. 34, Act No. 9 of 2022) | Do not collect data without a defined legal basis. Ensure every collection of data is mapped to one of the following:<br><br>1. Explicit consent (Schedule I, item a).<br><br>2. Contractual necessity (Schedule I, item b).<br><br>3. Legal obligation (Schedule I, item c).<br><br>4. Emergency (Schedule I, item d).<br><br>5. Public interest (Schedule I, item e).<br><br>6. Legitimate interest (Schedule I, item f). |
| **Section 6(1)** | "Every controller shall, ensure that personal data is processed for a— (a) specified; (b) explicit; and (c) legitimate, purposes and such personal data shall not be further processed in a manner which is incompatible with such purposes." (p. 4, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Clearly state why you are collecting data at the point of collection. Do not use this data later for other unrelated things unless you obtain separate consent.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Section 7**    | "Every controller shall ensure that personal data that is processed shall be— (a) adequate; (b) relevant; and (c) proportionate, to the extent as is necessary in relation to the purpose for which such data shall be collected or processed." (p. 5, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Only ask for what is strictly necessary. For example, if a user is signing up for an email newsletter, do not mandate their physical address, date of birth, or national identity card number.                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Section 8**    | "Every controller shall ensure that personal data that is processed shall be— (a) accurate; and (b) kept up to date, with every reasonable step being taken to erase or rectify any inaccurate or outdated personal data, without undue delay." (p. 5, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Build a simple profile update portal or self-service dashboard. Outdated mailing lists, wrong billing addresses, or incorrect credit files must be corrected or deleted immediately upon realization.                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Section 9**    | "Every controller shall ensure that personal data that is being processed shall be kept in a form which permits identification of data subjects only for such period as may be necessary or required for the purposes for which such personal data is processed: Provided however, subject to the provisions of section 10 of this Act, a controller may store personal data for longer periods in so far as the personal data shall be processed further for archiving purposes in the public interest, scientific research, historical research or statistical purposes." (p. 5, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                           | Establish clear data destruction cycles. Once a transaction is completed or a contract expires, securely purge or anonymize the data unless a legal requirement mandates storage. The archiving exception only applies to public interest, scientific, historical, or statistical purposes, not general business backup.                                                                                                    |
| **Section 10**   | "Every controller shall ensure integrity and confidentiality of personal data that is being processed, by using appropriate technical and organizational measures including encryption, pseudonymisation, anonymisation or access controls or such other measures as may be prescribed so as to prevent the  (a) unauthorized or unlawful processing of personal data; or (b) loss, destruction or damage of personal data." (pp. 56, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                        | Secure your tech stack. SMBs must implement Multi-Factor Authentication, encrypt customer databases at rest and in transit, enforce complex passwords, and restrict server access only to authorized staff.                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Section 11**   | "A controller shall, provide data subjects— (a) the information referred to in Schedule V; and (b) the information regarding any decision taken pursuant to a request made under PART II of this Act, in writing or by electronic means and in a concise, transparent, intelligible and easily accessible form." (p. 6, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Publish an accessible Privacy Policy on your website. Draft it in clear, non-legalese language and make sure it answers everything in Schedule V (who you are, why you collect data, and how users can withdraw consent).                                                                                                                                                                                                   |

## Pillar 4: Lawful Processing Conditions (Schedules IIV)

| Section                    | Direct Quote                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Application to SMBs                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Schedule I, Item (a)**   | "the data subject has given consent to the processing of his personal data;" (p. 54, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | The most common basis for SMEs. Use this for marketing email sign-ups, newsletter subscriptions, and any non-essential data collection. Remember: consent must comply with Schedule III conditions.                                                                       |
| **Schedule I, Item (b)**   | "processing is necessary for the performance of a contract to which the data subject is a party or in order to take steps at the request of the data subject prior to entering into a contract;" (p. 54, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Use this for order fulfillment, shipping addresses, payment processing, and employment contracts. You do not need separate consent for data that is essential to performing the agreed service.                                                                           |
| **Schedule I, Item (c)**   | "processing is necessary for compliance with a legal obligation to which the controller or processor is subject to under any written law;" (p. 54, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Use this for tax records (Inland Revenue), EPF/ETF contributions, regulatory filings, and anti-money laundering checks. You must retain this data for as long as the underlying law requires.                                                                             |
| **Schedule I, Item (d)**   | "processing is necessary to respond to an emergency that threatens the life, health or safety of the data subject or another natural person;" (p. 54, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Allows you to process data without prior consent in genuine emergencies. This is narrow and must be documented.                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Schedule I, Item (e)**   | "processing is necessary for the performance of a task carried out in the public interest or in the exercise of powers, functions or duties conferred, imposed or assigned on the controller or processor by or under any written law including any circular, direction or code issued by the government;" (p. 54, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Less commonly used by private SMEs, but applicable if you operate under a government licence or regulatory mandate.                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Schedule I, Item (f)**   | "processing is necessary for the purposes of the legitimate interests pursued by the controller or by a third party, except where such interests are overridden by the interests of the data subject which require protection of personal data, in particular where the data subject is a child." (p. 54, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Use this for fraud prevention, network security, internal analytics, and existing client relationship management. You must conduct a balancing test: your interest vs. the data subject's privacy. Extra caution is required where children's data is involved.           |
| **Schedule I, Item (g)**   | "for the purpose of item (e) of this Schedule, "public interest" includes— (i) processing of personal data is necessary for health purposes such as public health and social protection and the management of health care services; (ii) processing of personal data is necessary for the control of communicable diseases and other serious threats to health; (iii) processing of personal data is necessary by official authorities for achieving the purposes or objects laid down by law." (pp. 5455, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                  | If your SME operates in healthcare, pharmaceuticals, or public health supply chains, processing for disease control, health management, or official health mandates is explicitly authorized under public interest.                                                       |
| **Schedule I, Item (h)**   | "for the purpose of item (f) of this Schedule, "legitimate interest" includes— (i) processing in situations where the data subject is a client or in the service of a controller; (ii) whether a data subject reasonably expects at the time and in the context of the collection of the personal data that processing for that purpose may take place; (iii) processing of personal data is strictly necessary for the purposes of preventing fraud; (iv) processing of personal data to the extent strictly necessary and proportionate for the purposes of ensuring network and information security." (p. 55, Act No. 9 of 2022)                           | The Act gives explicit examples: existing client relationships, reasonable expectations, fraud prevention, and cybersecurity. Use these as benchmarks when conducting your balancing test. Document your assessment to prove compliance.                                  |
| **Schedule II, Item (a)**  | "the data subject has given consent, to the processing of special categories of personal data for one or more purposes specified by the controller at the time of processing, unless any other written law prohibits the processing of such personal data notwithstanding the consent of the data subject concerned. In the case of a child, consent shall mean the consent of the parent or legal guardian of such child;" (p. 55, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Processing sensitive data requires explicit consent for specified purposes. For children, only a parent or legal guardian can consent. Pre-ticked boxes are absolutely invalid here.                                                                                      |
| **Schedule II, Item (b)**  | "processing is necessary for the purposes of carrying out the obligations of the controller and exercising of the rights of the data subject, in the field of employment, social security including pension, and for public health purposes ensuring public safety, monitoring and public alert systems relating to impending health or other emergencies, the prevention or control of communicable diseases and other serious threats to public health and the management of public health-care services in so far as it is provided for in any written law providing for appropriate safeguards for rights of the data subject;" (p. 56, Act No. 9 of 2022) | You can process employee health records, pension details, and ETF/EPF contributions without explicit consent if it's required by employment law. Ensure you have appropriate safeguards in place.                                                                         |
| **Schedule II, Item (c)**  | "processing is necessary to respond to an emergency that threatens the life, health or safety of the data subject or another natural person where the data subject is physically or legally incapable of giving consent;" (p. 56, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | In medical emergencies where the data subject is unconscious or otherwise unable to consent, you may process their health/medical data. Document the emergency circumstances thoroughly.                                                                                  |
| **Schedule II, Item (d)**  | "processing relates to personal data which is manifestly made public by the data subject;" (p. 56, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | If a data subject voluntarily makes their sensitive information public, you may process it. Use cautiously and document the source.                                                                                                                                       |
| **Schedule II, Item (e)**  | "processing is necessary for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims before a court or tribunal or such similar forum, or whenever courts are acting in their judicial capacity;" (p. 56, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | If you need to process sensitive data to defend against or pursue a lawsuit, tribunal proceeding, or regulatory action, this basis applies. Retain litigation hold records accordingly.                                                                                   |
| **Schedule III, Item (a)** | "the controller shall demonstrate that the data subject has consented to processing of the personal data relating to such data subject;" (p. 57, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | You must be able to prove that consent was given. Implement timestamped consent logs recording the date, time, method, and specific scope of each consent. Verbal consent is insufficient without recorded evidence.                                                      |
| **Schedule III, Item (b)** | "if the consent of the data subject is given in the context of a written declaration which also concerns other matters, the request for consent shall be presented in a manner which is clearly distinguishable from the other matters, in an intelligible and easily accessible form, using clear and plain language: Provided that, such a declaration shall not constitute an infringement of any provisions of this Act." (p. 57, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Legal teams cannot bury consent clauses inside a general terms-of-service document. It must be presented separately, in clear language, and you must log/save a timestamp of when and how the user ticked the box to prove consent.                                       |
| **Schedule III, Item (c)** | "when assessing whether consent is freely given, special consideration shall be taken of whether, inter alia, the performance of a contract, including the provision of a service, is conditional on consent to the processing of personal data that is not necessary for the performance of that contract;" (p. 57, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Do not make purchase or service delivery conditional on consenting to unrelated data processing. If a customer buys a product, do not force them to consent to marketing analytics as a condition of the sale, consent would not be "freely given" and is therefore void. |
| **Schedule III, Item (d)** | "prior to giving consent, the data subject shall be informed thereof that consent can be withdrawn anytime subject to the provisions of this Act." (p. 57, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | At the exact moment you ask for consent, you must also inform the data subject that they can withdraw it at any time. This notice must be included on your consent forms, not buried in a separate policy document.                                                       |
| **Schedule IV, Item (a)**  | "processing of personal data relating to lawful investigations of offences or related security measures shall be carried out only in accordance with applicable written laws, whilst providing for appropriate safeguards for the rights and freedoms of data subjects;" (p. 58, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | If your SME handles data for law enforcement purposes, processing must comply with applicable criminal procedure laws and safeguard data subject rights.                                                                                                                  |
| **Schedule IV, Item (b)**  | "for the avoidance of doubt, processing of personal data may be considered lawful under this Schedule if investigations are carried out pursuant to the provisions of the Code of Criminal Procedure Act, No. 15 of 1979 or provisions under any other written law;" (p. 58, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Data processed pursuant to official criminal investigations under the Criminal Procedure Code is lawful. However, this does not grant blanket authority; appropriate safeguards must still be maintained.                                                                 |

## Pillar 5: Accountability & Program Management

| Section                  | Direct Quote                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Application to SMBs                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Section 12(1)**                       | "It shall be the duty of every controller to implement internal controls and procedures, (hereinafter referred to as the "Data Protection Management Programme") that— (a) establishes and maintains duly catalogued records... (b) is designed on the basis of structure, scale, volume and sensitivity... (c) provides for appropriate safeguards based on data protection impact assessments specified in section 24; (d) is integrated into the governance structure of the controller; (e) establishes internal oversight mechanisms; (f) has a mechanism to receive complaints, conduct of inquiries and to identify personal data breaches; (g) is updated based on periodic monitoring and assessments; and (h) facilitates the exercise of rights of data subjects under sections 13, 14, 15, 16 and 18..." (pp. 67, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                       | Scale your compliance program to your size. An SMB should draft an internal data registry cataloging what data is held, who can access it, and establish a designated internal folder for documenting data protection procedures. Your DPMP must include complaint handling mechanisms, periodic reviews, internal oversight, and rights facilitation workflows. |
| **Section 12(1)(a)**                    | "...establishes and maintains duly catalogued records to demonstrate the manner in which the implementation of the data protection obligations referred to in sections 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 and 11 are carried out by the controller;" (p. 6, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Keep an up-to-date catalog of your processing operations, listing: what data you process, the purposes, who it's shared with, retention periods, and security measures. This is the first compliance evidence the Authority will audit.                                                                                                                          |
| **Section 12(2)** (Repealed 2025)     | Original subsection repealed by Section 3 of Act No. 22 of 2025. (p. 1, Act No. 22 of 2025)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | The original mandatory guideline issuance by the Authority under subsection (2) was repealed. The DPMP framework is now governed by the broader Section 51A guidelines mechanism.                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Section 20(1)** (Amended 2025)      | "Every controller and processor shall designate or appoint a Data Protection Officer, to ensure compliance with the provisions of this Act, in the following circumstances:— (a) where the processing is carried out by a Ministry or government department... (b) where the core activities of processing carried out by the controller or processor consist of the following:— (i) operations which... require regular and systematic monitoring of data subjects on a scale and magnitude as may be prescribed; or (ii) processing of special categories of personal data on a scale and magnitude as may be prescribed; or (iii) categories of processing which results in a risk of harm affecting the rights of the data subjects protected under this Act, based on the nature of processing and its impact on the data subjects, as may be determined by the Authority by way of guidelines made under this Act." (pp. 56, Act No. 22 of 2025) | You do not automatically need a full-time, in-house DPO unless your core business revolves around systematic profiling, tracking location data, or handling bulk sensitive data. The 2025 amendment also expanded DPO triggers to include processing determined by Authority guidelines as high-risk.                                                            |

(p. 15, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | If you are required to have a DPO, publish their business email address clearly on your website's privacy page and register their details with the local Data Protection Authority.                                                                                                                                                                              |

(pp. 1718, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Before launching any high-risk system, you must complete a formal risk assessment. (p. 7, Act No. 22 of 2025)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Under the original 2022 Act, businesses had to proactively submit DPIAs to the Authority. (p. 7, Act No. 22 of 2025)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | If your DPIA flags a high risk, you must actively implement safety controls before launching the product.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |

(p. 7, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Consent must be as easy to withdraw as it is to give. Prior processing remains lawful.                                                                                                                  |
| **Section 14(2)**                      | "Every data subject shall have the right to request a controller in writing, to refrain from further processing of personal data relating to such data subject, if such processing is based on the grounds specified in items (e) or (f) of Schedule I or item (f) of Schedule II." (pp. 78, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | If you process a customer's data based on public interest (item e) or your "legitimate interests" (item f), you must immediately stop processing their data if they object in writing, unless you have overriding legal proof to continue.                                                                                                                           |
| **Section 15**                         | "Every data subject shall have the right to request a controller in writing to rectify or complete the personal data relating to such data subject which is either inaccurate or incomplete, and the controller shall, upon such a written request made by the data subject, rectify or complete the personal data without undue delay... Provided however, the provisions of this section shall not impose any obligation on a controller to collect and process any additional personal data that is not required for the purpose of processing: Provided further, where a controller is required to maintain personal data for the evidentiary purposes under any written law or on an order of a competent court, the controller shall refrain from further processing such personal data without rectifying." (p. 8, Act No. 9 of 2022)          | Ensure your customer service team is trained to handle "data correction" requests immediately. Correct database entries without delay. Note two important limits: (1) you don't need to collect new data just because a subject asks, and (2) if data is retained for evidentiary/court purposes, you must stop processing it but preserve it in its current form. |

(p. 2, Act No. 22 of 2025)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | The 2025 amendment expanded the response timeline from the original 21 working days to **one calendar month** and now also covers Section 18 (automated decision review) requests. Keep a close eye on requests to ensure you never miss this strict window.                                                                                                         |

You must assign reasons for the extension.                                                                                           |

(p. 2, Act No. 22 of 2025)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | You must formally notify the data subject of the extension and the specific reasons for it before the first month expires. Maintain a refusal log in your DPMP.                                                                                                                                                                                                            |

(pp. 11, Act No. 9 of 2022; p. 3, Act No. 22 of 2025)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | If your SMB uses fully automated software or AI algorithms to score applicants, you must provide users with a way to request a manual human review of automated decisions. | **Schedule V, Item 6**        | "Items 1 to 4 of this Schedule shall not apply where— (a) the controller has established the fact that the data subject has already been provided with or made aware of the information; or (b) the provision of such information proves impossible or would involve a disproportionate effort, in particular for processing for archival purposes in the public interest... subject to the conditions and safeguards provided in this Act... In such cases the controller shall take appropriate measures to protect the rights and freedoms of data subject... including making the relevant information publicly available; or (c) obtaining or disclosure is expressly laid down by any written law to which the controller is subjected to... or (d) the personal data shall remain confidential, consequent to obligations of professional privilege or is not permitted to be disclosed under any written law, including a statutory obligation of secrecy." (pp. 6061, Act No. 9 of 2022)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | You are exempt from providing Schedule V information only in four narrow cases: (a) data subject is already informed, (b) disclosure is impossible or disproportionate (e.g., archival research, but you must make info publicly available), (c) disclosure is mandated by a separate law, or (d) professional privilege or statutory secrecy applies. These are exceptions, not default positions.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
